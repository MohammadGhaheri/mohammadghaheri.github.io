About

Technology with an operational point of view.

My work sits between product, engineering and real-world operations. I focus on smart mobility, connected vehicles, telematics, IoT, logistics platforms, data products and the business models around them.

I am especially interested in problems where software has to work outside the lab: inside vehicles, fleets, transport networks and operational teams.

This site is my working notebook for practical findings, product thinking and lessons from building and operating technology in these environments.
