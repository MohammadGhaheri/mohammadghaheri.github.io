[Bijack](https://bijack.ir/) is an independent **open freight load board** for Iran's road-freight market. Shippers and load announcers can publish transport needs, while drivers can search available loads by route, city, province, cargo type and required vehicle body.

The underlying idea is simple: freight information should not remain trapped in scattered phone calls, crowded messaging groups and posts that disappear within minutes. When load information is structured, searchable and accessible, drivers have a better chance of finding relevant freight and shippers have a better chance of finding available capacity.

I built Bijack around that idea.

## What does Bijack actually do?

Bijack is not a transport company. It does not issue freight waybills and it does not replace licensed carriers, insurance requirements or the legal obligations attached to freight transport.

Its role is to **publish, structure, search and connect load information with available transport capacity**.

For a broader overview, the [About Bijack](https://bijack.ir/bijack-about/) page explains the product model in more detail.

In practice, there are two main sides of the market:

- **Shippers, companies, factories, warehouses, freight companies and load announcers** that want to publish freight demand
- **Drivers and fleet owners** that want to find loads that match their route and vehicle

There is also a dedicated [quick-start page](https://bijack.ir/loadboard/start/) for users who want to begin immediately.

## Publishing a load

If you have freight to move, you can use Bijack's [quick load announcement](https://bijack.ir/loadboard/announce/) page.

The core information for a load includes:

- Origin
- Destination
- Cargo type
- Required vehicle/body type

Additional details such as tonnage, freight price, visibility period, exact loading/unloading location and transport notes can also be added.

After the mobile number is verified, the load appears in the relevant load-board halls and related pages.

For light commercial transport, Bijack also provides a separate [pickup and light-truck load announcement](https://bijack.ir/loadboard/announce-pickup/) flow.

## Load-board halls are the core of Bijack's structure

One of the concepts that mattered to me from the beginning was the idea of **freight halls**.

Instead of putting every load in the country into one long, noisy list, Bijack organizes loads into specialized halls. The main directory is available on the [Bijack load-board halls](https://bijack.ir/loadboard/) page.

Halls can be organized by:

- Province
- Origin city
- Vehicle/body type
- Province and body-type combinations
- Special topics
- International routes

For example, the [Tehran Province load board](https://bijack.ir/loadboard/tehran/) shows loads originating in Tehran Province, while the [national box-truck load board](https://bijack.ir/loadboard/loader/room-truck/) collects loads relevant to that body type from across the country.

International freight is also separated into an [international transit load board](https://bijack.ir/loadboard/international/) for loads with foreign origins or destinations.

The point is to reduce noise: a driver should be able to go directly to the part of the market that matters to them instead of searching through a huge stream of unrelated freight.

## Searching by route and vehicle

Drivers can also skip the halls and use [Bijack load search](https://bijack.ir/loadboard/search/).

Loads can be filtered by origin, destination and required vehicle/body type. This is especially useful for a driver who already knows where they are departing from or who is looking for a return load on a specific route.

The Bijack homepage also includes a [live load board](https://bijack.ir/) showing recent freight announcements without requiring an initial search.

## Freight is only one side of the market

Road freight is not only about the question “where is the load?” There is another question: **which driver is available, and where?**

Bijack therefore allows drivers to announce that they are ready to carry freight. Shippers can browse [drivers ready to carry](https://bijack.ir/loadboard/ready-drivers/) and filter available transport capacity by route and vehicle type.

This makes the other side of the market visible: transport capacity that exists, but that a shipper may otherwise never discover.

## Bijack on mobile: Android, Cafe Bazaar, Myket and PWA

Bijack is not limited to desktop browsers. For users who work primarily from their phones, there are several official ways to access it.

The central [Bijack app download page](https://bijack.ir/download-bijack-app/) keeps the official options in one place. Android users can install **Bijack Open Load Board** directly from [Cafe Bazaar](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/ir.bijack.app) or [Myket](https://myket.ir/app/ir.bijack.app).

Users who prefer not to install an Android app, including iPhone users, can use the [Bijack PWA](https://bijack.ir/app/). On supported devices it can be added to the home screen and launched much like a native app.

The main mobile access options are:

- [Official Bijack app download page](https://bijack.ir/download-bijack-app/)

## No commission on the freight price

One of the product decisions behind Bijack is that it does **not take a percentage or commission from the freight price agreed between the driver and the load announcer**.

Bijack does not set the final transport rate. Instead, part of its business model is based on access to more load details.

Drivers can still view a limited number of load details without buying a package. Those who need broader access can use the [Bijack access packages](https://bijack.ir/loadboard/packages).

This distinction matters to me because Bijack is not designed to become a party to the transport transaction. Its job is to make discovery and connection easier.

## Bijack is not supposed to be everything

The boundary of responsibility is deliberate.

Bijack is a **platform for publishing freight information**. Before agreeing to any transport job, drivers and shippers still need to verify identity, cargo details, freight terms, insurance, permits, waybills and every other legal or operational requirement.

Finding freight and performing the transport legally and formally are not the same thing.

## Where did Bijack come from?

Bijack grew out of my earlier experience in Iran's freight market.

In previous projects, I had seen that building more software did not automatically make a market more digital. Sometimes a technically sophisticated product still depended on a large operations team constantly mediating between shippers, drivers and the software.

That led me to a different question:

> Could we build a system simple and accessible enough that users could use it themselves, without depending on a large operational organization?

Bijack grew out of that question, and its publicly traceable activity goes back to **November 28, 2021**.

I have written a longer version of that story on the [Bijack story](https://bijack.ir/bijack-story/) page.

## Bijack today

Based on the figures displayed on the Bijack homepage at the end of September 2026, the platform's historical and current records together included more than **95,000 registered drivers, more than 3,000 shippers/load announcers and more than 100,000 freight announcements**.

To me, these numbers are more than growth metrics. They point to a persistent market problem: a large amount of road-freight information is still fragmented, and there is real value in making that information structured and searchable.
