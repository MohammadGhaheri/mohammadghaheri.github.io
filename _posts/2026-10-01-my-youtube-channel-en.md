If I had to describe my YouTube channel in one sentence, it would be this:

**A place to talk about technologies that have to work in the real world.**

[My YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@MohammadGhaheri) originally grew around data, Power BI and analytics. Some of the earlier videos focused on practical topics such as Power BI, MariaDB, SQL Server and hands-on data work.

Over time, however, my professional work expanded well beyond BI tools, and the direction of the channel is expanding with it.

## From tools to data products

I have never seen Power BI, DAX, SQL or Python as isolated tools.

The more important question is how data becomes something that people can actually use in a decision, an operation or a digital product.

That means the channel will continue to cover topics such as:

- Power BI and Business Intelligence
- Data Analytics
- DAX, SQL and related tools
- Dashboard and data-product design
- Data architecture and implementation

But that is only one part of the story.

## When data enters the physical world

Another large part of my work involves systems where data does not come only from business software. It comes from **vehicles, devices, fleets and real operational environments**.

That brings topics such as:

- Internet of Things
- Telematics
- Connected Vehicles
- Fleet Management
- Smart Mobility
- Logistics Platforms
- Systems Integration
- Product Architecture

This is the part of technology I find especially interesting: the point where a device, a vehicle, an API, a data platform and a software product all have to work together.

## Why YouTube?

Some ideas fit well in an article or a LinkedIn post. Others need to be seen.

A dashboard should be demonstrated.

A system architecture is often easier to understand when it is drawn.

A data flow makes more sense when you can follow it step by step.

And sometimes the fastest way to explain a problem is simply to share the screen and show what is happening.

That is why I see YouTube as more than another social network. It is a good medium for **practical teaching, architecture walkthroughs and documenting lessons from real projects**.

## Not just tutorials

I do not want the channel to become only a collection of tool tutorials.

There will certainly be educational content, but I also want to explore larger questions:

- What makes a data product different from a dashboard?
- Why do some IoT projects stall after the hardware is installed?
- How can telematics and connected vehicles become revenue-generating services?
- What makes a fleet-management product genuinely usable?
- Where does product management fit into technical and operational systems?
- Why can a system be technically correct and still fail in the field?

These are questions whose answers usually do not live in a product manual.

## If you work with data, products or smart mobility

If you work in, or are curious about, **Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, IoT, Connected Systems, Smart Mobility, Telematics, Logistics or Product**, you will probably find something relevant on the channel.

My goal is to keep the content grounded in real work: less about buzzwords, more about how technology is actually designed, implemented and used.

You can find the channel here:

**[YouTube.com/@MohammadGhaheri](https://www.youtube.com/@MohammadGhaheri)**

If one video helps you solve a problem faster, discover a better product idea or avoid a mistake in a project, then the channel has done its job.
