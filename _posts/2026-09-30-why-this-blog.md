I spend a lot of time working on problems that sit between technology and operations. They rarely fit cleanly into one category.

A connected-vehicle product is not only an IoT project. A telematics platform is not only a data platform. A freight marketplace is not only software. Each one combines product decisions, operational constraints, commercial models, user behavior and technical architecture.

This blog is where I will publish the useful parts of that work.

## What I plan to write about

Expect practical notes on connected vehicles, fleet technology, telematics, smart mobility, logistics platforms, product strategy, data products, BI and IoT.

Some posts will be technical. Others will focus on product or business models. The common thread is simple: **what actually works in the real world, and why?**

I want this to be closer to a working notebook than a polished corporate publication.
